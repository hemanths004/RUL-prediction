Architectural Pipeline Highlights

01

Condition Clustering

KMeans unsupervised clustering (6 conditions for FD002 & FD004) enables condition-aware min-max normalization, removing operating regime variance.

02

Dual-Stream Feature Extractor

Multi-scale Conv1D (kernels 3, 5, 7) + 2-layer BiLSTM (hidden 128) + 3D Attention fused with 6 handcrafted statistical features (mean, slope, std, % dev, cusum, late slope).

03

4-Expert MoE Output Head

Specialized expert sub-networks combined via learned soft-gating with entropy regularisation and variance penalty to prevent expert collapse.

04

MC Dropout Uncertainty

50 stochastic forward passes at inference yield empirical predictive distributions, providing ±1σ uncertainty bounds and 90% confidence intervals.
